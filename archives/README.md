# Retained experimental evidence

These archives preserve the observations behind the published study, its
qualification controls and excluded collections. Offline replay checks
calculations and evidence identity; it does not make an excluded collection
eligible for scientific comparison.

| Group | Status | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `v2-linux-*` | Qualified emulator collections | Primary matrix, qualification, provider bypass and quota counterfactuals |
| `v2-independent-*` | Qualified independent CPU controls | Separate 4/8 CPU qualification and paired sensitivity comparisons |
| `cpu-native-baseline/`, `v2-ollama-fra6s004-cpu-continuation/` | Descriptive native baseline | All 45 planned observations, separate lifecycle regimes, 44 complete native certificates and the unchanged uncertain observation |
| `shared-admission/`, `v2-ollama-shared-admission/` | Complete paired native experiment | All 24 arms, twelve paired differences, native completion, refusals and reset evidence |
| `v2-model-pilot/`, `v2-ollama-fra6s004-cpu-confirmation-pilot/` | Separate calibration pilots | Workload selection and model-path observations, not additional comparison repetitions |
| `2026-11-12-*` | Historical calibration and focused observations | Earlier controls and contrasts, with eligibility retained per complete block |
| `v2-local-*`, `v2-remote-*`, `v2-ollama-isolated-*`, `v2-ollama-physical-network-diagnostics/`, `v2-model-control-interruption/` | Historical qualification, excluded or diagnostic collections | Preserve local controls, failed gates, interruptions and alternative explanations; never pool passing subsets of an excluded block |
| `v2-ollama-dell-cpu-memory-excluded/`, `v2-ollama-fra6s004-cpu-confirmation-planning/`, `v2-ollama-fra6s004-cpu-inspection-excluded/` | Operational provenance | Planning, readiness, source context and exclusions, not qualified trial results |
| `legacy-methods/`, `collected-protocol.md` | Captured methods | Original interpretation boundaries and collection protocol |

Each collection's manifest or evidence index binds its public files to their
compressed and decoded identities. Native collection inventories also preserve
the original export paths. Executed source bytes remain separately available
through the [instrument registry](../instruments/manifest.json).

An excluded or superseded collection can still explain a failed qualification,
an interpretation limit or a change in the apparatus. A removal candidate must
be checked against the manifests, source identities, checksums, replay commands
and published links. Preserve original observations and their recorded status;
new measurements use fresh paths.

The historical files tracked under [`runs/`](../runs/) are separately checksummed
raw evidence. Newly collected working runs and transfer capsules are ignored;
they are distinct from these public records.

See the [evidence index](../EVIDENCE.md), [validity limits](../VALIDITY.md)
and [offline reproduction guide](../docs/REPRODUCING.md).
