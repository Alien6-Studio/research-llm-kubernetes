# Validity and interpretation boundary

## Established within the qualified emulator apparatus

The 72-trial primary matrix, 12 provider-only controls, 12 quota controls and
48 fresh paired CPU trials reproduce from archived observations. Their separate
qualification blocks pass. The source, workload, image, pod membership, restart,
resource, planned-arrival deadline and provider-ledger checks define this scope.
Replicas improve useful outcomes with dependency headroom. Manipulating the
shared quota changes that relationship. The tested global policy has correct
atomic accounting but worse timely client outcomes in saturated conditions.

This is an exploratory mechanism study: three paired trace repetitions per
condition, 10-second arrival cohorts plus bounded drain, one-node kind, limited
workloads and finite offered load. Descriptive df2 Student intervals assume
normality/independence that three repetitions cannot establish. They are not
multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory tests, steady-state production capacity or
an equivalence result. Individual requests are not independent replications.

## Exclusions and alternative explanations

| Evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Original 21-trial pilot, both local legacy calibration blocks, local V2 4/8 CPU calibration | Whole blocks excluded; a passing subset never repairs qualification |
| GitHub primary environment versus independent LXD CPU environment | Analyze separately. Different source, hardware, scheduling and collection conditions prevent pooling |
| Paired 4/8 CPU controls on the 8-vCPU VM | Small observed differences weaken a CPU-only explanation here; uncertainty does not prove CPU irrelevance or hardware equivalence |
| Provider-only and 20k/40k/80k quota controls | Changing the suspected constraint moves outcomes here; not a universal exclusion of network, queue or CPU constraints |
| Sampled cgroup/VM/host observations | Retain missing values, steal and pressure. Sampling does not prove isolation or absence of short interference |
| Measured global lease policy | Four held active slots/worker, bounded 32-place queue, no deadline-feasibility scheduler. Negative results concern this policy, not every coordinated admission algorithm |

A useful task is HTTP 200 with a checkable correct result before its planned
client deadline. Emissions, attempts, provider consumptions and eventual answers
have different denominators. Late consumed work is never refunded into the
ledger and never becomes a useful outcome. Do not add separate stage quantiles
to describe a representative request. The published single-request illustration
is a rule-selected post hoc mechanism example, not a prevalence estimate.

Emulator units are declared consumption, not actual LLM tokens. Cohort throughput
uses arrivals followed through their deadline; completion-window throughput uses
finishes in the window. Resource costs integrate observed role traces, not
cloud billing or whole-system energy. No ROI, cost saving or HA benefit is measured.

## Native inference: completion and client usefulness are separate

The CPU native baseline retains all 45 frozen conditions. Of these, 44 have
complete native-completion certificates covering 1,490 native response bodies;
one original direct-model observation retains uncertain completion. The 1,490
responses are an integrity inventory, not independent statistical replications
or a throughput estimate.

All 28 previously unattempted conditions pass their individual collection,
resource and native-completion checks after refreshed transport controls and
all 12 qualification trials. Each new condition has a proved inference-process
restart and four technical warmup responses. Its 29 reset proofs and 112
technical warmup responses are replayed offline.

The original 17 observations share an inference epoch; their block remains
unqualified because the final observation lacks complete native termination.
The remaining 28 use a reset-per-trial lifecycle. These are different regimes.
They are not pooled into a 45-trial replica effect, confidence interval,
significance test or repaired qualified block. The failed observation was not
replaced, repeated or reclassified.

For the primary outcome, every planned measurement arrival remains in the
denominator over a 335-second cohort after a five-second arrival warmup. Useful
means correct HTTP 200 within ten seconds of the scheduled arrival. The native
certificate separately reconciles controller writes, terminal worker records,
provider admissions and complete native response bodies. A late native answer
cannot replace a timed-out client outcome.

The fixed task is a repetitive HTTP-status classification with a short JSON
label. These observations establish neither general model quality nor RAG
quality. CPU affinity and resource caps do not isolate shared memory bandwidth,
caches or unrelated host activity. A reset/warmup sequence does not establish
that lifecycle effects are absent. Per-seed arrival counts vary and must retain
their own denominators.

The native baseline has policy=none and a nonbinding emulated quota. It does not
test transfer of the emulator quota/admission interventions or demonstrate an
improvement from a new scheduling library. See the separate
[45-row report](analysis/v2-ollama-fra6s004-cpu-continuation/results.json).

## Shared native admission: a fixed cap and mixed outcomes

The separate [paired study](studies/shared-admission/PROTOCOL.md) completed its
24 trials: one/eight workers, near/high offered loads, three trace repetitions
and bypass/global admission. All trials pass capture and native-completion
checks after three fresh transport controls and twelve generator qualifications.
The retained evidence contains 25 proved resets, 96 technical warmup responses
and 844 complete native trial response bodies. Both arms use the same extended
instrument; every trial starts with a new native process and four common warmup
responses. These data remain separate from the 45-condition baseline and the
emulator comparisons.

The global cap stayed at one occupied generation in all twelve global arms.
Timely correct responses decreased in eleven paired comparisons and increased
in one. In the high-load/eight-worker group, repetition 0 changes from 0/71 to
17/71 useful arrivals; repetition 1 changes from 26/64 to 14/64 and repetition 2
from 35/44 to 13/44. All denominators include the complete scheduled measurement
cohort. Refusals, non-emissions and expired client requests remain failures; a
later completed model response never improves that client score. The
[full report](analysis/v2-ollama-shared-admission/results.json) preserves all
twelve differences, including the opposite direction in repetition 0.

The intervention combines a shared K=1 budget, immediate rejection, a permit
held until successful native HTTP completion and quarantine of uncertain work.
Its effect cannot be attributed to coordination alone. Even a single worker
changes from four local slots to one shared native permit. The fixed calibration
rule selects one despite failing to establish a ten-second p95 deadline at every
tested concurrency. K=1 is therefore a tested parameter, not an SLO-qualified or
optimal capacity recommendation.

Three pairs per worker/load condition support descriptive local differences;
there is no confirmatory significance test, request-level replication or pooled
estimate across the four conditions. Randomized paired order and identical
reset/warmup rules reduce order and carryover differences. They do not establish
steady state, independent repetitions or physical isolation from other host
activity. These results concern one CPU-hosted model, short classification task,
resource envelope and reset-per-trial lifecycle.

All 24 native lifetimes are certified. For an uncertain native outcome, the
protocol would retain a fully observed client cohort while censoring native
resource lifetimes and quarantining the permit until reset. Technical capture
defects stop collection without rerunning the arm. A client HTTP 429 alone does
not identify its cause: a worker can also refuse work locally. Shared-cap
refusals must be attributed from the admission ledger; its full-trace call
counts have a different denominator from measurement-arrival outcomes. Summed
native HTTP and held-permit lifetimes include waiting and are neither CPU time
nor per-client wasted work.

The measured cap does not estimate remaining task duration, prioritize by
client deadline or select an adaptive capacity. The results provide a negative
finding for a simple fixed policy in eleven pairs, rather than evidence that a
more elaborate scheduler or proposed production library will work. Sources and
all retained files are indexed in the [admission evidence](archives/shared-admission/evidence-index.json);
`scripts/check_shared_admission.py --mode final` replays them without Ollama.

## Historical actual-model exclusions remain excluded

The historical local 52-classification pilot is a closed-loop load-selection
measurement; its 4.689/s observed rate is not a production capacity estimate.
Its local 45-trial comparison remains excluded in full after 15 invalid trials,
despite passing preliminary model-sized zero-service controls.

The original remote metadata, compressed-metadata and 8 KiB fixture controls
each fail their unchanged 100 ms p95 gate. Other interrupted client/transport
and CPU-host campaigns remain separate excluded collections. These failures do
not establish a unique Mac, VM, GPU, network, host-memory or scheduler cause.
Later qualified transport and native observations do not repair those blocks.

## Unmeasured and prospective work

HPA/KEDA configurations and protocols are prepared only; there is no measured
comparison. No new autoscaling campaign is needed to publish this study's
restricted conclusions. No diminishing-return knee beyond eight replicas,
production SLO, general model capability, energy or monetary efficiency is established.

The data can motivate deadline-aware admission as an engineering requirement.
No proposed Alien6 production library or customer-system benefit has been
established. The completed native-admission comparison evaluates one fixed
research policy; the observed differences do not establish production efficacy.

## Instrument integrity

Captured source hashes identify the measured instruments for replay.
Current code restricts outbound destinations and legacy redirects. The archived
sources remain exact; no measured record is relabelled as using the correction.
The original cohort's full criteria remain in `archives/legacy-methods/`.
